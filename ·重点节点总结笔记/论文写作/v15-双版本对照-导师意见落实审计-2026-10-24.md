# 5. v15 里值得保留的好句子（改稿时别删掉）

1. §1 [9]：`The pseudo-infrared transform is deliberately not claimed as a contribution: it is a deterministic pixel-wise mapping whose only role is to give both modalities one verified box.` —— 一句话把导师的降格要求写在明面上，比藏在局限里更有说服力。
2. §4.1 [72]：`no detector was trained on CDFF output and evaluated on real imagery, so nothing in this paper supports a claim of improved detection accuracy` —— 前置声明，避免审稿人读到最后才发现。
3. §4.4 [107]：主动撤回旧稿 81.5% 的那两句 —— 这是全稿可信度最高的一处，保留。
4. §5 [130]：`Constrained closed-loop feedback is the weakest of the three: … it does not improve quality over four cycles, so it is offered as a bounded mechanism with a measured outcome rather than as a demonstrated advantage.` —— 第三贡献的正确写法。
5. §6 [137]：`until they are run, CDFF should be read as a method for producing and screening spatially controlled synthetic data, not as a demonstrated route to better detectors.` —— 结论段的定位句，符合导师原话。
