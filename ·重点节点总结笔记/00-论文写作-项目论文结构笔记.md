> ⚠️ **关键事实**：生成管线不依赖任何公开 UAV 检测数据集。DroneMMset 既不是 LoRA 训练源，也不是背景池来源。Anti-UAV410 仅作为下游评估基准。这是论文的**卖点**而非弱点——数据依赖极低意味着方法可迁移性强。

> 🎯 **数据集欠缺论证点（2026-08-14 确立，用于 Related Work / Motivation）**：两个分析基准暴露了现有公开 LSS 数据集的缺口——
> - **DroneMMset**：RGB+IR 视频齐全，但官方 bbox 标注**不准确**（疑似 RF 定位转换，非精确视觉标注）→ 标注质量欠缺；
> - **Anti-UAV410**：**纯热红外（无 RGB）** → 模态不完整。
>
> 两者共同反衬「合成数据生成」的价值：真实采集要么标注不准、要么模态不全，而生成管线可产出**像素级对齐的 RGB-IR-BBox 三图一体**。

> 📌 **方向调整记录（2026-08-14 会话确立）**：
> 1. **Scope 扩展为多类低慢小目标**：生成对象从固定 4 类拓展为「多类低慢小目标」，写作以此为准。叙事为「一个通用管线 + 可插拔 LoRA」——语义理解确定生成对象 → 引用对应 LoRA 模型 → 其余（背景 / ControlNet / IR 转换 / 验证端 / 循环机制）完全一致。
> 2. **架构从「7-Agent」改为「双 Agent 对抗」**：生成端 Generator Agent（内部 7 个功能模块 modules）+ 审查端 Validator Agent（V1-V9）。背景匹配、IR 转换等确定性环节不称 Agent。
> 3. **实验演示内容假设**（⏳ 待后续针对性补充）：前端对生成段每个步骤的图进行展示（2×5 十图已实现）；审查端（Validator）的分析与矫正内容假定已有，之后补充具体素材。

### 3.1 Introduction（引言）— 标准四段式

#### 第 1 段：问题定义

**要写的**：
- 低慢小目标（无人机/风筝/气球/飞艇）检测是低空安防的核心挑战
- 真实数据采集成本极高：多天气×多时段×多场景组合爆炸
- 三个核心挑战：
  1. **长尾场景覆盖严重不足**：雨天/雾天/沙尘/逆光等极端环境样本稀缺
  2. **公开数据集视角错配**：大部分 UAV 数据集是无人机**俯拍地面**，而非地面**仰拍低空目标**
  3. **非 UAV 类别数据完全空白**：风筝/气球/飞艇全球无公开检测数据集

**关键文献引用**：Anti-UAV410 (TPAMI 2023), DroneMMset (Scientific Data 2025)

#### 第 2 段：现有方法分类 + 不足

分成 3 类方法，每类指出缺陷：

| 方法类别 | 代表性工作 | 缺陷 |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **纯 prompt 驱动生成** | DetDiffusion, FLORA, AeroGen | 无法精确控制目标空间位置和尺度，不适合大规模数据集构建 |
| **手工条件图驱动** | ControlNet, T2I-Adapter, Multi-ControlNet | 需要人工绘制 depth/seg/pose 条件图，不可自动化 |
| **简单贴入合成** | Copy-Paste augmentation | 边缘生硬，光照/纹理不一致，缺乏场景融合 |

→ "现有方法都有问题" → 引出本文方案

#### 第 3 段：本文方案概述

**要写的**（不用公式，纯文字描述）：

> 本文提出 [框架名]，一个面向低慢小目标的端到端数据集构建框架。核心思路是「**先标记空间位置，再让 ControlNet 按场景上下文重绘**」：

1. 用户输入自然语言描述（如"阴天下午，四旋翼从远处飞近，仰拍"）
2. LLM 解析为结构化双 JSON，从 37,852 张真实背景图库自动匹配背景
3. 提取背景深度和分割图 → LoRA 生成无人机 → 抠图 → silhouette **标记注入** depth/seg 条件图
4. ControlNet 以注入后的 depth+seg 为双条件，对无人机区域进行**局部重绘** → 融合自然的 RGB 输出
5. RGB → 白热红外伪彩色转换 → RGB-IR 配对数据
6. 10 产物全可观测：bg / bg_depth / bg_seg / drone_raw / drone_clean / drone_depth / drone_seg / final_rgb / bbox / ir
7. 9 阶段多循环 Validator（V1-V9）在生成管线的每一步拦截错误 → 失败码精确路由到出错 Agent → 三个独立 Buffer 积累 → 触发增量微调 → Generator 与 Validator 在物理锚定约束下协同进化

#### 第 4 段：Contributions 列表

> (1) We propose [框架名], the first end-to-end framework that combines **marker injection + ControlNet dual-condition repainting** for automated low-slow-small target dataset generation. Unlike prior methods that either rely on hand-crafted condition maps or suffer from imprecise spatial control, our approach injects drone silhouettes into depth/segmentation maps as spatial markers, enabling ControlNet to repaint the drone region with scene-contextualized textures.

> (2) We introduce a **37,852-image real-world background pool** with CLIP-based classification (9 scenes × 3 time-of-day × 6 weather), replacing synthetic backgrounds with authentic ones. This is the first systematic comparison between real and synthetic backgrounds in the context of data augmentation for object detection.

> (3) We design a **9-stage multi-loop validation chain** (V1-V9) with precise failure-to-agent routing. The input-side pre-validation (V1-V5, 19 rule-based checks) intercepts errors before and during costly diffusion inference; the output-side review (V6-V9) assesses quality, scene consistency, trajectory continuity, and detectability with short-circuit evaluation. Crucially, each validation failure is routed to the exact pipeline component responsible, forming **closed feedback loops**: V1→Agent 1 (LLM), V2→Agent 2 (background matching), V3-V5→Agent 3 (depth/seg/LoRA/Focus), V8→Agent 1 (trajectory learning) + frame interpolation.

> (4) We implement a **Focus rapid repositioning mechanism** that decouples drone placement from full pipeline regeneration — repositioning a drone takes seconds instead of minutes, enabling interactive spatial refinement without re-running LoRA generation, rembg segmentation, or background analysis.

> (5) Through systematic **ablation studies across four dimensions** (marker injection, multi-stage validation, closed-loop feedback, and curriculum hardening), we demonstrate the necessity and contribution of each component. We further show that the multi-loop adversarial training paradigm — where the Generator and Validator engage in constrained adversarial optimization under physical rule anchors **with automatic threshold escalation, hard-negative recalibration, and diversity penalties (V_diversity)** — produces higher-quality and more diverse training data than unidirectional generation pipelines.

#### paper organization 段落

The remainder of this paper is organized as follows. Section 2 reviews related work in synthetic data generation (§2.1), spatial controllable generation (§2.2), low-slow-small target detection (§2.3), and quality assessment with closed-loop feedback in generative pipelines (§2.4). Section 3 introduces our proposed framework, covering the LLM-driven semantic parser, background matching and scene extraction, LoRA-based drone generation with ControlNet repainting, RGB-to-IR domain conversion, the 9-stage multi-loop quality validation chain, and the multi-loop adversarial feedback mechanism with curriculum hardening. Section 4 presents comprehensive experimental results, including quantitative comparisons with state-of-the-art methods, qualitative visualizations, and ablation studies across four dimensions. Section 5 discusses the contribution of each component, limitations of the current approach, and promising directions for future work. Section 6 concludes the paper.

**末尾引出 → Gap for RQ4**：
Despite rapid progress, existing synthetic data generation methods exhibit three systematic weaknesses: (i) they operate on **full-image synthesis**, where background and foreground are jointly generated, precluding precise object-level control; (ii) they employ **single-stage filtering** that cannot attribute failures to specific pipeline components; (iii) they lack **cross-modal (RGB+IR) extension**, producing single-modality data incompatible with thermal infrared detectors. Our framework addresses all three: localized repainting (§3.4) decouples foreground from background, 10-stage validation (§3.6) pinpoints errors by component, and RGB-to-IR conversion (§3.5) generates paired dual-modal data.

覆盖从 ControlNet 到 layout-conditioned 方法的空间控制技术演进，聚焦**条件图自动化**这一核心 gap。

|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| [16] | **ControlNet** (Zhang et al., ICCV 2023) | 冻结 SD 权重的可训练副本，接受 depth/seg/pose/canny/hed 空间条件 | 本文核心机制基座 |

**末尾引出 → Gap for RQ1**：
All existing controllable generation methods share a critical bottleneck: they require **manually crafted condition maps** (hand-drawn depth, segmentation, or pose). For large-scale dataset construction — where thousands of images across diverse scenes, weather, and trajectories are needed — manual annotation is infeasible. Our key innovation is **automated condition map generation via marker injection**: the system creates marked depth and segmentation maps with embedded drone silhouettes directly from natural language scene specifications, eliminating manual condition map creation while preserving precise spatial control through ControlNet dual-condition repainting.

Our framework targets this gap by generating diverse, precisely annotated training data across **four LSS categories × six weather × four time periods × dual modalities**, evaluated on Anti-UAV410 TIR benchmark as a held-out test bed.

**末尾引出 → Gap for RQ2 + RQ3**：
Existing evaluation methods operate as **stateless, one-pass filters**: they accept or reject a generated image based on a quality score but (a) cannot attribute failures to upstream pipeline components, (b) cannot trigger targeted corrections, and (c) do not adapt to observed failure patterns. Similarly, existing multi-agent frameworks (DiffusionAgent, MAIR) use homogeneous agent populations for peer review, not heterogeneous, pipeline-stage-specific validators. Our contribution is threefold: (i) a **heterogeneous 10-stage validation chain** where each validator specializes in one pipeline boundary (V1→LLM output, V2→background match, V3-V5→intermediate generation products, V6-V10→final output quality/diversity); (ii) **short-circuit evaluation** that terminates the chain at first failure, saving downstream GPU inference; (iii) **failure-to-agent routing** enabling a curriculum-hardened adversarial feedback loop that closes the gap between generation and validation — transforming the pipeline from a unidirectional production line into a self-improving system.

##### 3.3.3 Marker Injection Strategy

这是整个框架最核心的创新。与现有方法的对比：

| 方法 | 效果 |
|:--|:--|
| 全图合成 (ControlNet v5 旧方案) | 背景不可控，每次生成不同背景 |
| 简单贴入 (Copy-Paste) | 边缘生硬，光照/纹理不一致 |
| **标记注入 + CN 重绘 (本文)** | silhouette 写入 depth/seg → ControlNet 把无人机当作"场景一部分"来重绘 → 融合自然 |

**具体实现**：

```python
# Step 3: silhouette 注入
drone_depth = bg_depth * (1 - weight_map) + drone_depth_val * weight_map
drone_seg[drone_mask > 0] = class_id_drone (7)
```

- depth 注入：Gaussian 软阈值 (weight>0.15)，物理混合 `bg×(1-w) + drone_depth×w`
- seg 注入：drone mask 区域直接赋值 class_id=7（红色）
- σ 缩放：`clamp(400/d, 3, 12)`，近大远小
- 深度推导：`1/(1+d/20)`，30m→0.36, 100m→0.17

**核心公式**：

Given background depth map \(D_{bg} \in \mathbb{R}^{H \times W}\) and segmentation map \(S_{bg} \in \{0,...,6\}^{H \times W}\), drone silhouette mask \(M \in \{0,1\}^{H \times W}\), and target depth value \(d_{val}\) derived from the trajectory specification:

\[
W(x,y) = G_{\sigma} * M(x,y), \quad \sigma = \text{clamp}\left(\frac{400}{d}, 3, 12\right)
\tag{1}
\]

where \(G_{\sigma}\) is a Gaussian kernel with standard deviation \(\sigma\) and \(*\) denotes convolution. The soft weight map \(W\) creates a spatially smooth transition zone around the drone silhouette, with blur radius inversely proportional to target distance \(d\) — closer drones (larger in frame) receive sharper edges, while distant drones blur more naturally into the background.

The marked depth and segmentation maps are then:

\[
D_{marked}(x,y) = D_{bg}(x,y) \cdot (1 - W(x,y)) + d_{val} \cdot W(x,y)
\tag{2}
\]

\[
S_{marked}(x,y) = 
\begin{cases}
c_{drone}, & M(x,y) > 0 \
S_{bg}(x,y), & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
\tag{3}
\]

where \(c_{drone} = 7\) is the reserved class index for drone objects, visually rendered as red in the segmentation map. The depth value \(d_{val}\) is derived from the trajectory-specified distance \(d\) (in meters):

\[
d_{val} = \frac{1}{1 + d/20}
\tag{4}
\]

This yields physically plausible depth ratios: 30m → 0.36, 50m → 0.29, 100m → 0.17.

- `blend_seed` 加 10 万避免碰撞

**ControlNet 双条件引导重绘公式**：

The ControlNet img2img repainting step operates under dual spatial conditions (marked depth \(D_{marked}\) and segmentation \(S_{marked}\)) with the alpha-composited initial image \(I_{init}\) as the starting latent. At each denoising timestep \(t\), the noise prediction is conditioned on text prompt \(c_{text}\) and both spatial conditions:

\[
\hat{\epsilon}_t = \epsilon_\theta(z_t, t, c_{text}, D_{marked}, S_{marked})
\tag{5}
\]

Following classifier-free guidance, the final noise prediction interpolates between conditional and unconditional predictions:

\[
\tilde{\epsilon}_t = \epsilon_\theta(z_t, t, \emptyset) + w \cdot \left(\epsilon_\theta(z_t, t, c_{text}, D_{marked}, S_{marked}) - \epsilon_\theta(z_t, t, \emptyset)\right)
\tag{6}
\]

where \(w\) is the guidance scale (default 7.5). The latent is updated via DDIM sampling:

\[
z_{t-1} = \sqrt{\alpha_{t-1}} \cdot \frac{z_t - \sqrt{1-\alpha_t} \cdot \tilde{\epsilon}_t}{\sqrt{\alpha_t}} + \sqrt{1-\alpha_{t-1}} \cdot \tilde{\epsilon}_t
\tag{7}
\]

Importantly, the repainting is **local**: the denoising strength \(\gamma = 0.65\) (controlled by `blend_strength`) determines the degree of modification relative to \(I_{init}\). The drone region receives heavy denoising (integrating scene lighting, texture, and atmospheric effects) while the background remains largely untouched — the ControlNet conditions ensure the repainted drone conforms to the spatial layout defined by the marked depth and segmentation maps.

**产物**：10 个输出 slot（见 [[#3.1 Overview]]），全部 Web 前端可视化。

#### 3.5 RGB-to-IR Domain Conversion (Agent 6)

**实现**：`rgb2ir_converter.py`

```
RGB 图像 → 灰度化 → 反转（白热）→ 三通道重建（B×0.85 微蓝调）→ IR 伪彩色
```

**为什么有效**：转换后三通道有色差 → SD 1.5 VAE 可正常编解码 → 无域不匹配问题。对比：IR 背景 LoRA 训练失败（VAE 无法编码三通道完全相同的 IR 灰度图）。

**转换公式**：

Given an RGB image \(I_{RGB} \in \mathbb{R}^{H \times W \times 3}\), we compute the grayscale luminance channel \(Y\) using the standard luma coefficients:

\[
Y(x,y) = 0.299 \cdot R(x,y) + 0.587 \cdot G(x,y) + 0.114 \cdot B(x,y)
\tag{8}
\]

The white-hot infrared pseudo-coloring inverts the luminance (brighter objects appear hotter) and reconstructs three channels with a subtle blue tint for VAE compatibility:

\[
I_{IR}(x,y) = \begin{bmatrix}
0.85 \cdot (1 - Y(x,y)) \
1 - Y(x,y) \
1 - Y(x,y)
\end{bmatrix}
\tag{9}
\]

The 0.85 coefficient on the blue channel introduces a slight blue cast that mimics the spectral characteristics of mid-wave IR sensors, while the inter-channel variance (B ≠ G = R) ensures the SD 1.5 VAE encoder produces a non-degenerate latent representation. This is critical: our early experiments with pure grayscale IR (three identical channels) caused VAE encoding collapse, making IR-specific LoRA training impossible. The pseudo-coloring strategy resolves this while preserving the essential visual features (shape silhouettes, edge gradients) that object detectors rely on.

**局限**（诚实声明）：colormap 映射非基于真实热辐射物理，但对目标检测训练（关注形状和边缘）影响可控。

#### 3.6 9-Stage Multi-Loop Quality Validation (Validator Agent)

The validation chain is organized into two tiers: **input-side / in-generation pre-validation (V1-V5)** that intercepts errors before or during costly GPU inference, and **output-side review (V6-V9)** that evaluates the final dual-modal output with short-circuit evaluation.

##### 3.6.1 Input-side and In-Generation Validation (V1-V5)

19 rule-based and lightweight signal-processing checks, zero training dependency, distributed across the v7.2 pipeline:

| Stage | Pipeline Step | Checks | Method | Failure Code |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| **V1** | Agent 1 (LLM) → dual JSON | 5: schema completeness, enum validity, logic self-consistency, trajectory bounds, camera parameter range | Pure rule engine | `V1_SCHEMA_INCOMPLETE`, `V1_LOGIC_CONFLICT`, etc. |
| **V2** | Agent 2 (Background Match) → background image | 3: scene_type label match, CLIP semantic similarity, image basic validity (no corrupted/blank images) | Rule + CLIP ViT-B/32 | `V2_SCENE_MISMATCH`, `V2_SEMANTIC_LOW` |
| **V3** | Agent 3 Step 1 (Depth/Seg) → bg_depth + bg_seg | 4: depth value validity, seg non-empty, sky ratio reasonable, depth-seg coarse alignment | Rule + lightweight statistics | `V3_DEPTH_INVALID`, `V3_SEG_EMPTY` |
| **V4** | Agent 3 Step 2 (LoRA Gen) → drone_raw | 4: LoRA concept bleed (SSIM vs blank), overfitting texture repeat (autocorrelation), global color cast (channel stats), drone non-empty | SSIM + autocorrelation + channel statistics | `V4_CONCEPT_BLEED`, `V4_TEXTURE_REPEAT` |
| **V5** | Agent 3 Step 5 (Focus) → final_rgb (repositioned) | 3: bbox position offset < tolerance, scale match with drone_scale, bbox within canvas bounds | Rule (coordinate validation) | `V5_POSITION_DRIFT`, `V5_BBOX_OOB` |

**Key design rationale**: V1-V5 are strategically placed at the outputs of individual pipeline steps. V1 catches semantically inconsistent JSON *before* any GPU work begins. V2 prevents a mismatched background from entering the expensive ControlNet pipeline. V3-V5 catch issues within Agent 3's sub-steps before they propagate to the final output, enabling targeted sub-step re-execution rather than full pipeline restart.

##### 3.6.2 Output-side Review (V6-V9)

| Stage | Aspect | Method | Decision Logic |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| **V6** | Dual-modal image quality | RGB: BRISQUE (primary failure criterion); IR: pixel range + contrast + FFT mid-frequency (advisory only) | `BRISQUE > thr` → FAIL. IR anomalies log warnings but never cause failure (deterministic code conversion) |
| **V7** | Drone-scene consistency (4D) | D1: bbox-to-distance geometric rule; D2: KL divergence of RGB histograms (drone crop vs. background); D3: IR bbox size comparison; D4: cross-modal IoU > 0.95 | Any dimension fails → component-specific feedback |
| **V8** | Trajectory continuity (4 physical rules) | R1: position continuity; R2: size continuity; R3: acceleration constraint; R4: direction smoothness | Pure physics rules. Note: R3/R4 failures trigger dual-path repair rather than simple rejection (see §3.8) |
| **V9** | Detection validity | YOLO inference on RGB (primary criterion) + IR (reference only); IoU matching with GT bboxes | Undetected GT bbox → FAIL. This is the **most important feedback signal** for LoRA fine-tuning |
| **V10** | Diversity constraint | CLIP feature extraction on drone crop → FAISS nearest-neighbor retrieval against archived features; cosine similarity check | max_sim ≥ 0.95 → FAIL (`MODE_COLLAPSE`); 10 consecutive frames > 0.90 → FAIL (`LOW_DIVERSITY`). Prevents Generator from collapsing to easy-to-pass modes |

**Short-circuit evaluation**: V6→V7→V8→V9 execute sequentially. Any stage failure terminates the chain and routes the failure code to the corresponding agent.

**Design principle**: Inspiration drawn from multi-agent systems (DiffusionAgent, MAIR), but adapted for a *heterogeneous* architecture where validators are not peer agents but specialized guards at each pipeline boundary. Unlike ODGEN/FLORA/InstaGen which filter outputs only once, our multi-stage validation provides **granular failure attribution** — each failure code identifies not just *what* failed, but *which component* is responsible and *what corrective action* to take.

##### 3.6.3 Core Validation Metrics (Formal Definitions)

**V6: Image Quality via BRISQUE.** The Blind/Referenceless Image Spatial Quality Evaluator (BRISQUE) operates on the Mean Subtracted Contrast Normalized (MSCN) coefficients of image \(I\):

\[
\hat{I}(x,y) = \frac{I(x,y) - \mu(x,y)}{\sigma(x,y) + 1}, \quad \mu(x,y) = \sum_{k=-K}^{K}\sum_{l=-L}^{L} w_{k,l} \cdot I(x+k, y+l)
\tag{10}
\]

where \(\mu\) and \(\sigma\) are the local mean and standard deviation computed over a Gaussian-weighted window \(w\). The MSCN coefficients are modeled as a Generalized Gaussian Distribution (GGD), and the pairwise products of neighboring MSCN coefficients as an Asymmetric GGD (AGGD). The 36-dimensional feature vector (2 GGD parameters + 16 AGGD parameters) is fed to a pre-trained SVR regressor trained on the LIVE database. A frame is rejected if:

\[
\text{BRISQUE}(I_{final}) > \tau_{BRISQUE}
\tag{11}
\]

with the initial threshold \(\tau_{BRISQUE} = 35\) (subject to L1 curriculum escalation, see §3.8.6).

**V7: 4-Dimensional Scene Consistency.** Beyond the bbox-to-distance geometric rule (D1), the histogram consistency check (D2) measures the KL divergence between drone crop and background RGB distributions:

\[
D_{KL}(P_{drone} \| P_{bg}) = \sum_{b \in bins} P_{drone}(b) \cdot \log\frac{P_{drone}(b)}{P_{bg}(b)}
\tag{12}
\]

where \(P_{drone}\) and \(P_{bg}\) are the 256-bin normalized RGB histograms of the drone crop and the surrounding background region, respectively. A high KL divergence indicates the drone appears visually disconnected from its scene context.

**V10: Diversity Constraint via CLIP Feature Similarity.** To detect mode collapse, we extract a CLIP ViT-B/32 feature vector \(f_{new} \in \mathbb{R}^{512}\) from each new drone crop \(I_{drone}\) and retrieve the maximum cosine similarity against all previously archived drone crops \(A = \{I_1, ..., I_N\}\):

\[
\text{sim}(f_{new}, f_k) = \frac{f_{new} \cdot f_k}{\|f_{new}\| \cdot \|f_k\|}
\tag{13}
\]

\[
\text{V10\_FAIL} \iff \max_{k \in A} \text{sim}(f_{new}, f_k) \geq \theta_{collapse}
\tag{14}
\]

where \(\theta_{collapse} = 0.95\). A secondary warning threshold \(\theta_{warn} = 0.90\) is monitored over a sliding window of 10 consecutive frames. FAISS IndexFlatIP enables approximate nearest-neighbor search with sub-millisecond latency per query.

#### 3.8 Multi-Loop Adversarial Feedback Mechanism

The core insight of our framework is that the Generator and Validator form a **constrained adversarial relationship**: the Generator aims to produce frames that pass validation, while the Validator aims to accurately distinguish useful from useless data. Unconstrained, this leads to reward hacking (Goodhart's Law). Our solution: **physical rule anchors + isolated validator updates + human-in-the-loop calibration**.

##### 3.8.1 Failure-to-Agent Routing Table

Each of the 9 validators routes failures to the **exact pipeline component and sub-step** responsible:

| Validator Failure | Responsible Component | Feedback Target | Corrective Action |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| V1 JSON errors | Agent 1 (LLM) | Agent 1 | Return field list + contradiction description → LLM regenerates with corrected prompt |
| V2 BG mismatch | Agent 2 (Background Match) | Agent 2 | Try next candidate from the 37,852-image pool (3 fallback attempts) |
| V3 Depth/Seg issues | Agent 3 Step 1 | Agent 3 Step 1 | Re-extract with adjusted parameters or new background |
| V4 LoRA artifacts | Agent 3 Step 2 | Agent 3 LoRA | Retry with new seed/CFG (3 attempts) → accumulate negatives → LoRA incremental FT |
| V5 Focus drift | Agent 3 Step 5 | Agent 3 Step 5 | Recompute offset matrix |
| V6 Low quality | Agent 3 Step 2 | Agent 3 LoRA | Adjust CFG/seed/steps → accumulate → LoRA FT |
| V7 Consistency fail | Agent 3 Step 4 (CN Repaint) | Agent 3 CN | Adjust blend_strength/cnet_scales |
| V8 Trajectory: R1/R2 (position/size) | Agent 1 (LLM) | Agent 1 | Regenerate trajectory JSON |
| V8 Trajectory: R3/R4 (accel/direction) | Agent 1 (LLM) | Agent 1 | ① LLM negative-sample learning (avoid similar violations) ② Frame interpolation smoothing |
| V9 Undetectable | Agent 3 Step 2 | Agent 3 LoRA | Negative sample accumulation → LoRA incremental FT (🔥 most critical) |

##### 3.8.2 Three Independent Feedback Buffers

To prevent cross-contamination between components, failures are dispatched to three isolated buffers:

| Buffer | Validator Sources | Threshold | Training Target |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| **LoRA-Buffer** | V4 + V6 + V9 | 50 samples | Agent 3 LoRA incremental fine-tuning (paradigm B: contrastive learning) |
| **CN-Buffer** | V3 + V7 | 30 samples | Agent 3 ControlNet-LoRA (rank=4) contrastive fine-tuning |
| **LLM-Buffer** | V1 + V8 | 20 samples | Agent 1 prompt optimization via negative-sample learning |

##### 3.8.3 Adversarial Isolation Constraints

The Generator-Validator adversarial loop is governed by five non-negotiable rules:

1. **Validator never learns from Generator outputs**: Validator updates use only human-annotated data
2. **Validator updates are far less frequent** than Generator improvements
3. **V8 physical rules are immutable anchors**: physical formulas cannot be bypassed; only the repair mechanism (LLM learning + frame interpolation) expands
4. **Independent real test set as ultimate arbiter**: downstream mAP on held-out real data is the final judge
5. **Human always in the loop**: every Validator calibration requires human confirmation

##### 3.8.4 Trajectory Repair: Dual-Path Design for V8

A key innovation in our feedback design is the **non-binary handling of trajectory failures**. While pure position/size anomalies (R1/R2) indicate incorrect scene JSON and require LLM regeneration, acceleration and direction violations (R3/R4) admit two complementary repair strategies:

1. **LLM negative-sample learning**: Accumulated violation patterns are fed back to prompt templates, enabling the LLM to learn to avoid generating physically implausible trajectories
2. **Frame interpolation**: Intermediate frames are inserted between consecutive keyframes, distributing abrupt changes across smaller increments and smoothing the trajectory

This dual-path design distinguishes our framework from prior work that treats trajectory validation as a binary pass/fail gate. The physical rules themselves remain immutable — our contribution is the *repair mechanism that adapts to different failure modes*.

##### 3.8.5 Relationship to Prior Work

Unlike ODGEN, FLORA, and InstaGen — which treat quality filtering as a one-time, unidirectional operation — our multi-loop feedback mechanism creates **multiple, component-specific optimization cycles** within the generation pipeline. The key difference is granularity: where prior work either accepts or rejects entire outputs, we attribute failure to specific components and enable targeted correction. This is akin to multi-agent reinforcement learning with specialized critic networks, but with the critical addition of external physical anchors that prevent reward hacking.

The three-buffer architecture with staggered thresholds (20/30/50) ensures that only **consistent failure patterns** — not isolated noise — trigger model updates, preventing the catastrophic forgetting and mode collapse that plague naive online learning approaches.

##### 3.8.6 Validator Curriculum Hardening (对抗升级)

A fundamental risk in any adversarial training setup is that the Validator remains static while the Generator continuously improves. Over time, the Generator learns to exploit the Validator's fixed decision boundary, producing frames that artificially inflate pass rates without genuine quality improvements (Goodhart's Law). Our solution is a **three-layer curriculum hardening** mechanism that escalates the Validator's standards in lockstep with Generator improvements:

**L1: Automatic Threshold Escalation.** When a Validator's pass rate exceeds a calibrated trigger (e.g., V6 BRISQUE > 90%), its acceptance threshold is automatically tightened (e.g., BRISQUE < 35 → < 30). Each threshold has a hard physical boundary (e.g., BRISQUE ≥ 18) that prevents runaway tightening. If downstream mAP drops more than 2%, the escalation is rolled back.

Formally, let \(\tau_k^{(t)}\) be the threshold for validator \(V_k\) at escalation step \(t\), with physical lower bound \(\tau_k^{\min}\). The escalation rule is:

\[
\tau_k^{(t+1)} = 
\begin{cases}
\tau_k^{(t)} - \Delta\tau_k, & \text{if } \text{pass\_rate}(V_k, W_t) > \theta_{trigger}^{(k)} \text{ and } \tau_k^{(t)} - \Delta\tau_k \geq \tau_k^{\min} \
\tau_k^{(t)}, & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
\tag{15}
\]

where \(W_t\) is a monitoring window (default 100 frames) and \(\theta_{trigger}^{(k)}\) is the validator-specific trigger threshold. The rollback condition is:

\[
\text{ROLLBACK} \iff \text{mAP}_{downstream}(\tau_k^{(t+1)}) < \text{mAP}_{downstream}(\tau_k^{(t)}) \cdot (1 - \delta_{mAP})
\tag{16}
\]

with \(\delta_{mAP} = 0.02\). The per-validator parameters are:

| Validator | \(\tau_k^{(0)}\) | \(\tau_k^{\min}\) | \(\Delta\tau_k\) | \(\theta_{trigger}^{(k)}\) |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| V2 (CLIP similarity) | 0.22 | 0.15 | 0.02 | 0.90 |
| V4 (SSIM bleed) | 0.35 | 0.20 | 0.03 | 0.88 |
| V6 (BRISQUE) | 35 | 18 | 3 | 0.90 |
| V7 (KL divergence) | 0.40 | 0.25 | 0.03 | 0.88 |

**L2: Hard-Negative Recalibration.** As the Generator improves, borderline samples (frames scoring near the acceptance threshold) are collected and periodically reviewed by a human annotator. Misclassified borderline samples become hard negatives for incremental fine-tuning of the Validator (EfficientNet backbone frozen, only classification head updated). The Validator thus learns to discriminate increasingly subtle failure modes.

**L3: Diversity Penalty (V_diversity).** The most subtle form of reward hacking is mode collapse: the Generator produces frames within a narrow, easily-validated manifold. We add a diversity constraint that extracts CLIP features from each drone crop and performs FAISS nearest-neighbor retrieval against the archived pool. Frames with cosine similarity ≥ 0.95 to an existing archived frame are rejected as mode collapse (`V10_MODE_COLLAPSE`); ten consecutive frames with similarity ≥ 0.90 trigger a low-diversity warning. This constraint requires no training and adds only ~15ms per frame on GPU.

The combined effect of these three layers transforms the Generator-Validator relationship from unidirectional optimization into genuine **adversarial co-evolution**, where both parties grow stronger under the oversight of immutable physical anchors (V8) and an independent held-out test set.

##### 3.8.7 Algorithm: Curriculum Hardening Scheduler

```
Algorithm 2: Validator Curriculum Hardening Scheduler

Input: validator thresholds T = {τ₁,...,τ₄}, escalation config C,
       monitoring window W (size=100), held-out test set D_test,
       detector M
Output: updated thresholds T', escalation log L

 1:  for each monitoring cycle over W do
 2:      for each validator k ∈ {V2, V4, V6, V7} do
 3:          pass_rate[k] ← count(passed) / |W|             ▷ step 1: monitor
 4:
 5:          // ── L1: Threshold Escalation ──
 6:          if pass_rate[k] > C.θ_trigger[k] then
 7:              τ_candidate ← τ_k - C.Δτ[k]
 8:              if τ_candidate ≥ C.τ_min[k] then
 9:                  τ_k_old ← τ_k
10:                  τ_k ← τ_candidate
11:                  mAP_old ← evaluate(M, D_test)
12:                  mAP_new ← evaluate_with_threshold(M, D_test, τ_k)
13:                  if mAP_new < mAP_old · (1 - C.δ_map) then
14:                      τ_k ← τ_k_old                            ▷ Rollback
15:                      log_rollback(k, τ_candidate, mAP_new, mAP_old)
16:                  else
17:                      log_escalation(k, τ_k_old, τ_k)
18:
19:      // ── L2: Hard-Negative Recalibration ──
20:      B ← collect_borderline(W, T)           ▷ |V_k_score - τ_k| < ε
21:      if |B| ≥ C.min_borderline then
22:          B_labeled ← human_annotate(B)
23:          Validator_EfficientNet ← incremental_FT(B_labeled)
24:          log_recalibration(|B|, |B_labeled|)
25:
26:      // ── L3: Diversity Monitoring ──
27:      for each frame f ∈ W do
28:          f_clip ← CLIP(f.drone_crop)
29:          sim_max ← max(cosine_sim(f_clip, f_archived) for f_archived in archive)
30:          if sim_max ≥ C.θ_collapse then
31:              reject(f, MODE_COLLAPSE)
32:      if consecutive_low_diversity(W, C.θ_warn, window=10) then
33:          trigger_warning(LOW_DIVERSITY)
34:
35:      W ← slide_window(W, size=C.window_size)
36:
37:  return T', L
```

Key design decisions: L1 escalation and rollback operate on **held-out** data only (never in-place on training data), L2 recalibration requires human annotation (no auto-labeling of borderline samples), and L3 diversity checking adds negligible overhead (~15ms/frame) since CLIP features are computed once per drone crop and archived.

## 七、写作避坑清单

1. ❌ Related work 不要只是罗列论文 → ✅ 每段结尾必须批判 + 引出本文
2. ❌ 公式不要只放不管 → ✅ 每个公式后追加符号定义
3. ❌ 实验不要只放表 → ✅ 每个表后必须有分析段落
4. ❌ Introduction 不要先罗列贡献再讲方法 → ✅ 先讲方法再列贡献
5. ❌ Limitation 不要回避 → ✅ 诚实说局限，为未来工作造势
6. ❌ 图表不要无引用 → ✅ 每个图/表至少在正文引用一次
7. ❌ 不要说"持续学习" → ✅ 说"Iterative Quality Bootstrapping"或"Self-Refining Generation Pipeline"
8. ❌ 不要过度强调全自动化 → ✅ 强调"固定任务空间内的自主精炼"，人在环中做战略决策
9. ❌ 不要回避非 UAV 类别的验证困难 → ✅ 坦诚定性展示 + 说明 UAV 强校准泛化逻辑
10. ❌ 不要忽略 IR 转换的物理局限性 → ✅ 在 Limitation 中明确声明 colormap 映射的边界
